# Research

Research is offline and read-only relative to trading execution. Generated
datasets and reports live under the ignored `data/research/` directory.

Strategies and hypotheses are not automatically approved for Paper or Testnet.
Research Data Engine v2 and V4C leverage-stress research are currently in
progress. Their generated datasets, manifests, and reports remain ignored.

Future canonical research documentation will include architecture, datasets,
strategies, a durable research log, and a tools index. News/Event and Social
Intelligence are future research directions, not runtime capabilities.

The migration of `app/tools/README.md` remains pending until the current
in-progress research diff is resolved; this index does not change that file.
